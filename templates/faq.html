Help

Questions and Answers

Quick answers about ordering, timing and care. Still wondering? Send us a message.

Ordering

How does customizing work?

Pick a style, choose a color, then choose your sizes. Add your design by uploading a file, choosing one from My Designs, or picking one from our gallery. You will see it on the shirt before you order.

Can I upload my own design?

Yes. A clear, high-resolution PNG or JPG works best. When you upload, you confirm you have the right to use the artwork. If we notice a quality problem with your file, we will contact you before we print.

Can I use a design from the gallery?

Yes. Open the Design Gallery, choose a design, then pick the shirt it goes on. Want a change, like a different name or color? Send us a message and we will adjust it for you.

Can I add something to the back?

Yes. A back design is $3 for just a name or just a number, and $6 for both, or for artwork on the back.

Timing and shipping

How long does an order take?

Everything is made to order, so please allow 2 to 3 weeks. Most orders are finished in about a week and a half, and production usually starts within 1 to 3 business days after your order is confirmed.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is a flat $11. Pickup is also available at checkout. You will get a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

Can I get my order faster?

If you have a date to hit, send us a message before you order. We will tell you honestly what is possible.

Returns and fixes

What if something is wrong with my order?

Because every order is custom made, we cannot accept returns or exchanges unless there is a defect or a printing error on our part. If something is not right, contact us within 7 days of receiving it and we will make it right.

What if I ordered the wrong size?

Tell us as soon as you notice. Changes may not be possible once production has started, so it is worth double-checking your size before you order. Our size guide can help.

Will the colors match my screen?

Very close, but not always exact. Screens differ, and printing on fabric always has small variations. We do our very best to match.

Sizing and care

How do I choose a size?

Our size guide lists chest and length for each brand, measured flat. Between sizes? Send us a message and we will help.

How do I care for my shirt?

Wash it inside out in cold water. Tumble dry low or hang dry. Do not iron directly on the print.

Group and company orders

How do group orders work?

Choose the styles and your logo, then share one link. Everyone picks their own size and pays their own way, and we print and sort the whole order. See group orders.

Can a company pay for its team?

Yes. With Company Apparel, your company covers a set number of items per person and pays one invoice. Anyone who wants extras pays for those themselves. Learn more.
