Who We Are

Purposefully Made KC was born from a simple belief: custom apparel should feel as good as it looks, and ordering it should be just as enjoyable. We are a small Kansas City business, and every order is made just for you.

We print with DTF (Direct-to-Film) on garments from Bella+Canvas, Comfort Colors, District, Independent Trading Co. and MV Sport, so colors stay vibrant and prints last wash after wash.
