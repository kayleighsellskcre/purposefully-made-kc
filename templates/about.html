Who I Am

I started Purposefully Made KC on a simple belief: custom apparel should feel as good as it looks, and ordering it should be just as enjoyable. This is a small Kansas City business, and I make every order just for you.

I print with DTF (Direct-to-Film) on garments from Bella+Canvas, Comfort Colors, District, Independent Trading Co., MV Sport, Port & Company and Rabbit Skins, so colors stay vibrant and prints last wash after wash.
