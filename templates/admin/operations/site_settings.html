Emails to customers

When you move an order to Packaged & Ready, Shipped or Completed, the customer gets a short, kind email. It says where to pick it up, that it is going home with a child, or that it shipped. Fifteen days after an order is completed, they get one more email inviting a review and photos.

Group stores with their own pickup spot use that one instead.

Kansas sales tax rate

This is the combined rate (state, county and city) for your Shawnee pickup address. Ask your CPA or look up the address with the Kansas Department of Revenue, then enter it here. It applies to pickups, hand-offs and orders shipped within Kansas. Orders shipped to another state are not charged Kansas tax. Shipping itself is not taxed.

A $100 item would be charged ${{ '%.2f'|format(tax_percent) }} in tax. New orders use the new rate right away; past orders keep the rate they were charged.

How long an order takes
