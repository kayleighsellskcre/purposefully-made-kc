Common Questions

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum. One shirt or one hundred — we print what gets ordered.

How does payment work?

Each person pays individually at checkout (card, Apple Pay, PayPal, Venmo). You don't collect money or deal with Venmo requests.

How long does it take?

Typically 5–10 business days from when the order closes. We'll confirm your timeline when you set up the order.

Can I restrict what people can order?

Yes — you choose which styles, colors, and designs the team can pick from. Great for keeping everyone matching.

Can I do pickup instead of shipping?

Yes. Set a pickup address and instructions when you create your group order. Shipping is also available (+$11).
